# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| No dispositions appear | The cloud connection is down or the license is missing | Check the AMP cloud connection and the license |
| Detailed analysis reports are not available | The management center is not associated with your Secure Malware Analytics account | Associate the account and allow up to 24 hours |
| A file stays on a host after a malware verdict | The firewall does not quarantine endpoint files | Use Secure Endpoint policy to quarantine |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
