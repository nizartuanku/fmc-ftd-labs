# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| A change does not take effect | A higher layer overrides it | Check the layer order and the setting in each layer |
| Recommendations enable nothing useful | Network discovery has no host data | Let discovery collect hosts first |
| Another policy changed unexpectedly | A shared layer was edited | Check which policies share the layer before editing |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
