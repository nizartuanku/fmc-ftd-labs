# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot register the FDM device to FMC | It is still in local management mode | Switch management to the management center first |
| Expected a setting in FDM that is missing | FDM exposes a simplified feature set | Check whether the feature needs FMC |
| Device version not accepted by cloud-delivered FMC | Unsupported version such as 7.1 | Upgrade to a supported version |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
