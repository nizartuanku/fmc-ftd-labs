# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Remediation status shows failure | pxGrid is down or ISE has no ANC policy | Check the pxGrid client status and ANC configuration |
| Nothing happens | The correlation policy is inactive or the rule does not match | Check the policy slider and rule conditions |
| ISE-PIC deployment | ISE-PIC cannot perform EPS remediations | Use a full ISE deployment |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
