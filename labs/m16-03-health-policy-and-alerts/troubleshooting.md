# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| No data for a device | No health policy deployed to it | Apply and deploy a health policy |
| A module shows no data after an upgrade | New modules were not reapplied | Reapply the health policy |
| Status changes but nobody is notified | No health monitor alert or broken alert response | Check Monitor Alerts and the response target |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
