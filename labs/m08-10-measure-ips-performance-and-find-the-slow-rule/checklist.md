# Verification checklist

Work through this after the test steps. Do not assume it works: confirm each item.

- [ ] Engine version and platform capacity were known before tuning.
- [ ] A baseline of performance statistics was recorded.
- [ ] The slow rule was identified from data, not guessed.
- [ ] Custom rules have strong content matches before PCRE.
- [ ] Deployment warnings about Snort restarts were reviewed.
- [ ] `show asp drop` and health monitoring were checked after the change.
