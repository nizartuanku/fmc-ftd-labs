# Verification checklist

Work through this after the test steps. Do not assume it works: confirm each item.

- [ ] Change management is enabled and every change is tied to a ticket.
- [ ] The preview was reviewed before each deployment; no unexplained pink entries remain.
- [ ] Out-of-band changes were reconciled in the management center, not just acknowledged.
- [ ] `show running-config interface` and `show route` show the expected values after deployment.
- [ ] Deployment history shows who changed what.
