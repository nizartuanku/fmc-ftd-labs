# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| A local device change disappears after a deployment | It was made outside the management center and then overwritten | Reconcile it in the management center before deploying |
| Change management cannot be disabled | Tickets are still open | Approve or discard them first |
| Unexplained pink entries in the preview | Configuration on the device would be removed or disassociated | Investigate before approving |

---
*Always confirm behavior against the guide for your exact FMC/FTD version.*
